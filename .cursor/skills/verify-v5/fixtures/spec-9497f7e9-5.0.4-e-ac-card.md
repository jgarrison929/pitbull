- **Actual cost card: one cutoff.** Ruling 2026-10-27 (PT), on the 24-118 gap ("actual cost didn't rise after the superintendent's approval"). The job page Actual cost (AC) card (`job-actual`) shows AC at the locked cutoff, the same basis as CPI and EAC. Its figure does not move when a report is approved, only when the next period is locked. [#88](https://github.com/jgarrison929/pitbull/pull/88) made the card live (cutoff plus since-lock) in `app/app/jobs/[number]/page.tsx`, and that change is rejected. The one-cutoff rule in `v5/tests/e2e/approve-return.spec.ts` ("Cutoff AC (last lock) — does not move until the next PM lock") and `v5/tests/e2e/earned-value.spec.ts` ("One cutoff: tile AC stays at last lock; approved cost shows as since-lock") stands, and both files say so on `main` at `59cbf2d` and on `rc/5.0.4` at `c62f053`.
- **Since-lock line on the AC card.** When cost posted after the latest lock is more than $0, the card adds a second line that reads exactly `+$X posted since Period N lock`. `$X` is the amount with cents (`usdCents`), and `Period N` is the latest locked period, for example "+$1,240.00 posted since Period 2 lock". The line is `data-testid="job-ac-since-lock"` with `data-cents=<amount>` on that same element. When the amount is $0, or less, the line is not rendered. The foreman never sees it (the no-dollar-delta rule in 5.0.4-b). Every other role that sees the AC card sees it: superintendent, PM, and executive.
