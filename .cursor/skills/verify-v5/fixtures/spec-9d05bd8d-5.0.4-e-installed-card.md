- **Installed card: claimed quantity plus a since-lock line.** Ruling 2026-10-27 (PT), from Verify's failed `--rc` on `ab74c4f3` (25-031 cost item 04-210). The cost-item page's Installed card headline (`cost-item-installed`) stays at the claimed quantity, as the i-2 table defines it, for example "248 CY" on 04-210 after **Reset demo**. Approving a report does not move it; the next lock does. When quantity posted after the latest lock is more than 0, the card adds a line that reads exactly `+X UNIT posted since Period N lock`. `X` is the quantity as displayed (`qty()` in `lib/ev.ts`), `UNIT` is the item's unit, and `Period N` is the latest locked period, for example "+12 CY posted since Period 2 lock". The line is `data-testid="cost-item-qty-since-lock"` with `data-qty-milli=<quantity × 1000>` on that same element. At 0 the line is not rendered. Every role sees it, the foreman included: a quantity is not a dollar delta. No foreman text rule in this spec matches it: "+12 CY" has no `$`, so `/[−-]\$\d/`, `/-\$/`, and `/At risk/` do not match, and neither does 5.0.6 J25's `/[+−-]\$\d/`. It counts as at most one secondary figure, so the card stays within the 2-secondary limit in e-1 and i-2.
