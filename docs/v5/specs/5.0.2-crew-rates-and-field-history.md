## Why this increment

[Fresh-start plan section 4](../fresh-start-plan.md#4-500-core-the-smallest-credible-ineight-style-story) step 5 defines AC as hours × crew rates (equipment rates are out of 5.0; see the 2026-10-24 note in that section) and asks for drill-down to daily reports. Section 8 asks for crews and rates and 30 to 60 days of approved daily reports.

On `main` at `0ddae8da` (and on #699 without Part A of this spec):

- Every labor hour still costs $85.00 (`DEFAULT_LABOR_RATE_CENTS` in `v5/src/lib/ev.ts`). Finding D6 in [cost and earned value intel](../intel/cost-and-earned-value.md).
- `#699` adds `crews` with **no** rate column.
- The seed still has two daily plans and one "Crew labor" cost entry per item (`draftCosts` in `v5/src/seed/index.ts`).
- The job page stops at the cost-code row.

InEight costs labor as hours × each employee's hourly rate (field intel C19). 5.0.2 uses one blended rate per crew.

## User stories

1. As the PM, I see that labor on 25-031 costs Bridge Crew 2's rate, and the rate is printed on each labor row.
2. As the superintendent, when I approve a report, the cost that posts uses the crew's rate, and the approve summary shows the arithmetic.
3. As the PM or the executive, I open a cost item from the job page and see every daily report and invoice that makes up its actual cost. The totals equal the job page.
4. As the PM, I open one daily report and see its lines, its crew and rate, who approved it, and every step it went through, including a return and its reason.
5. As the PM, I see every change on a job, not only the last 20.

## Part A: crew labor rates

### Rules

- Add `labor_rate_cents integer NOT NULL` on `crews`. There is no rate column in #699; this migration is `0014_crew_labor_rate.sql` (or the next free number after 0013 lands).
- `approveDailyReport` and `getApprovalPendingByCostItem` in `v5/src/server/progress.ts` join `daily_plans.crew_id` → `crews.labor_rate_cents`. They stop reading `DEFAULT_LABOR_RATE_CENTS`.
- Each posted labor `cost_entries` row sets `rate_cents` to that crew rate. The rate is a snapshot. A later rate change does not rewrite posted rows.
- Delete `DEFAULT_LABOR_RATE_CENTS` from `v5/src/lib/ev.ts`. One function owns the lookup:

```ts
// v5/src/server/plans.ts (add next to listCrews from #701)
export declare function laborRateForPlan(
	tx: Tx,
	planId: string,
): Promise<number>;
```

- `listCrews` gains `laborRateCents` in its select list so the seed and tests can assert it. No screen edits rates in 5.0.2.
- Dual-write of `daily_plans.crew` text stays as in #699/#701. Do not drop the text column in 5.0.2.

## Part B: seeded field history

### Rules

- For each active job (24-118, 25-031, 24-077), the seed writes approved daily plans on each working day, Monday to Friday, for the **30 working days** before the seed date (`seedNow()`).
- Each historical plan sets both `crew_id` and legacy `crew` text (same dual-write as #701's `createDailyPlan`).
- Lines cover cost items whose planned dates span that day. For each units cost item, reported quantities across approved reports sum to `installed_qty_milli`. Reported man-hours sum to the item's seeded labor hours. Hours stay the same as 5.0.0. Only the rate changes.
- Each approved line has one labor `cost_entries` row, dated with the plan date, `source_plan_line_id` set, `rate_cents` from the crew. Remove the single "Crew labor" entry from `draftCosts`. Keep material, equipment, and subcontract invoice rows.
- One historical report on 25-031 was returned with reason **Recount pour 3** and approved the next day.
- Seed is still a pure function `(companyId, today) → rows`. It writes rows. It does not call `run()` for the 30-day history.
- Replay test (vitest): for at least one plan per job plus the returned Cedar plan, replay through `createDailyPlan` → `addPlanLine` → `dailyPlan.submit` → report → `dailyPlan.approve` (the same path #701 and M3 use). Fail if amount, rate, date, or cost item differ from the seeded rows.

### Schema additions on `daily_plans`

Live `dailyPlan.create` from #701 must set `createdById` to the actor. Report sets `reportedAt`.

- SR-99 (24-118): |actual − earned| / earned < 0.15.

## Part C: drill-down and full change list

### Routes

| Route | Who | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| `/app/jobs/[number]/cost-items/[code]` | All 4 roles | CB, EV, AC, installed qty, earned MH vs actual MH. Table of every `cost_entries` row: labor (date, crew name, MH, rate, amount, approver) and other (date, note, amount). Totals row. |
| `/app/jobs/[number]/plans/[id]` | All 4 roles | Date, crew, foreman, rate, state. Lines with planned and reported qty/MH. History from plan columns + return reason. |
| `/app/jobs/[number]/changes` | All 4 roles | Full job change list from `jobChanges`, 50 per page. Job page **Changes on this job** links here. |

- Cost-code rows on the job page link to the cost item page. Labor rows link to the plan page.
- Totals use `v5/src/server/cost-rollups.ts` (no third copy of EV math; finding D11).
- Plan page is read-only. Approve, return, and report stay on the job page.
- Empty cost item (for example 05-120 with no field labor): written guidance **"Labor posts here when a daily report for this cost item is approved"**, not "No reports yet".
