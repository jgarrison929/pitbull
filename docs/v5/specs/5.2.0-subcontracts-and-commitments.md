## Why this is first in 5.2

Every later 5.2 increment reads a commitment: the Committed Cost forecast (5.2.1), pay applications (5.2.2), and sub change orders (5.2.3). 5.2.0 proves the commitment numbers on staging before any forecast moves. InEight's contract record carries line items on WBS codes and an SOV (C6, C11), and executing it feeds committed cost to Control (C18). Pitbull keeps one record with lines on cost items (recommendation 1).

## User stories

1. As the PM, I open Cedar Creek (25-031) and see its subcontracts, each with vendor, state, retention rate, and total.
2. As the PM, I draft a subcontract with SOV lines on cost items, and nothing is committed until I execute it.
3. As the PM, I execute a subcontract, and the cost items show the new commitment and the Current Budget still uncommitted.
4. As the PM, when a commitment would exceed a cost item's Current Budget, I am told by how much and that the executive must execute it.
5. As the executive, I execute an over-budget subcontract with a reason, and the warning is recorded.
6. As the superintendent, I see which subs are under contract on my job, read-only.
7. As anyone, I cannot edit an executed subcontract on screen or through the assistant.

## 5.2.0-a: schema, money math, seed

**Migration: 0024** (`0024_subcontracts.sql`).

**Files.** `v5/src/db/schema.ts`: `subcontract_status` and `subcontract_command` enums, and tables `vendors`, `subcontracts`, `subcontract_lines`, `subcontract_events` (PRD data model sketch). New `v5/src/lib/subcontract.ts`: `lineAmountCents`, `SUBCONTRACT_TRANSITIONS`, `formatScNumber`. Seed rows. No UI. No assistant actions.

### Rules

- A vendor name is unique per company, ignoring case (`lower(name)` unique index).
- A subcontract belongs to one job and one vendor. `number` is unique per job and shows as `SC-001`.
- `retention_bp` is basis points, 0 to 2,500 (0.00% to 25.00%), enforced by a check constraint.
- `subcontract_lines.qty_milli` > 0 and `unit_price_cents` > 0 (check constraints). A lump sum is quantity 1, unit `LS`.
- `amount_cents` = round half-up of `qty_milli × unit_price_cents ÷ 1000`, computed in `lineAmountCents` with `BigInt`, never floats. The writer stores it. A check test recomputes every seeded line.
- A line's unit is its own. It does not have to match the cost item's unit (C11). Commitments are money, so nothing sums quantities across units.
- `subcontract_events` has a unique index on `subcontract_id` where `to_status in ('executed', 'voided')`, so one subcontract gets one decision, as `change_order_events_decision_uq` does for change orders.
- Transitions: `create` → `draft`; `edit` draft → draft; `execute` draft → executed; `void` draft → voided. Nothing leaves `executed` or `voided` in 5.2.0.

## 5.2.0-b: server module, typed actions, commitment read

**Files.** New `v5/src/server/subcontracts.ts`, the only writer of the four tables. `subcontract.*` keys in `server/assistant/actions.ts` and the router. `commitmentByCostItem(jobId)` and job totals in `server/cost-rollups.ts`.

### Rules

- Only the PM creates, edits, and voids. The PM or the executive executes. The superintendent and the foreman are refused, with "Couldn't …" wording on the rail.
- A draft-estimate job refuses every subcontract action with "Approve the estimate before adding subcontracts."
- A line's cost item must be a leaf on the same job. A summary code is refused with "05 is a summary code. Pick a cost item under it." A code from another job is refused with "There is no cost item 07-150 on 25-031."
- Execute needs at least one line. A subcontract with no lines is refused with "SC-003 has no lines to commit."
- **Over Current Budget.** At execute, for each cost item on the subcontract: other executed commitment on that item + this subcontract's lines on it is compared with the item's total Current Budget (sum of `cb_*`). If it is higher, the PM is refused with "Commitment on 06-200 would exceed Current Budget by $27,600.00. An executive must execute it." The executive may execute it with a reason. The event stores `over_cb_cents` and the message shown (C20, recommendation 4).
- The total Current Budget is used, not `cb_sub_cents`. Seeded jobs keep their budget as one lump in labor (`estimate_source = 'seed'`), so the sub category would read $0.00 there.
- **Executed is final.** Edit, add line, remove line, and void on an executed subcontract are refused with "SC-001 is executed and cannot be edited." A second execute is refused with "SC-001 is already executed." and writes nothing (C27).
- **Stale saves.** Each command names the `revision` it showed. A stale revision is refused and names the user who changed it, as change orders do.
- Every accepted command writes one `subcontract_events` row in the same transaction, with actor, role, time, from and to state, revision, and total. The job change list (`jobChanges`) shows it.
- Vendor: `subcontract.create` takes a vendor name. An existing name (ignoring case) is reused. A new name creates the vendor in the same transaction.
- **Commitment read.** `commitmentByCostItem(jobId)` sums `amount_cents` of lines on executed subcontracts, grouped by cost item. One query per job page. Uncommitted CB = Current Budget − commitment.
- No forecast reads a commitment in 5.2.0. `itemForecast` and `eacSql` are unchanged.

### Typed actions

All writes go through `run()` with `writes: true`.

| Action | Arguments | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `subcontract.create` | `job`, `vendor`, `title`, `retentionPercent` | pm |
| `subcontract.edit` | `job`, `sc`, fields (`vendor`, `title`, `retentionPercent`), `revision` | pm |
| `subcontract.addLine` | `job`, `sc`, `costCode`, `description`, `unit`, `quantity`, `unitPrice`, `revision` | pm |
| `subcontract.editLine` | `job`, `sc`, `line`, fields, `revision` | pm |
| `subcontract.removeLine` | `job`, `sc`, `line`, `revision` | pm |
| `subcontract.execute` | `job`, `sc`, `revision`, `reason` (required when over Current Budget) | pm, executive |
| `subcontract.void` | `job`, `sc`, `reason`, `revision` | pm |
| `job.commitments` | `job` | pm, executive, superintendent (read) |

The assistant maps "Execute SC-002 on 25-031" to `subcontract.execute` and "What's committed on 25-031?" to `job.commitments`.

## 5.2.0-c: screens

**Files.** Routes `/app/jobs/[number]/subcontracts` and `/app/jobs/[number]/subcontracts/[sc]` (for example `/app/jobs/25-031/subcontracts/SC-001`). A Commitments tile and a Subcontracts section on the job page. Commitment rows on `/app/jobs/[number]/cost-items/[code]`. Nav entry. Visual baselines.

### Rules

- **Who sees what.** PM: full screens with edit and execute on drafts. Executive: the same reads, plus execute. Superintendent: read-only list and detail, no buttons. Foreman: no Subcontracts nav entry. A foreman who opens the URL is redirected to the job page.
- Harbor (26-010) has no Subcontracts nav entry and no section until its estimate is approved.
- **List.** One row per subcontract: number, title, vendor, state, retention, total, and committed amount ($0.00 for a draft). Executed first, then drafts, then voided.
- **Detail.** Header with vendor, state, retention, and total. One row per line: L-number, cost item code and description, description, quantity, unit, unit price, amount. The event history with actor, role, and time.
- **Job page.** A Commitments tile: total commitment and the number of executed subcontracts, linking to the list. It sits beside the cost tiles and does not change CPI, SPI, or EAC.
- **Cost item page.** Rows for Commitment and Uncommitted Current Budget, and the subcontract lines on that item with links. A negative uncommitted amount reads "Over Current Budget by $27,600.00".
- No label, tab, or button for pay applications, retention held, sub change orders, or close-out. Retention shows as a rate only.
- Every page gets a purpose line (5.0.4-b `PageHeader` `purpose` prop). Subcontracts list: "Who is under contract on this job, and how much is committed." Detail: "The lines this subcontract commits, by cost item."
- At 320 and 390 px, lists stack as cards with one main number (the total), per 5.0.4-c `MetricCard`. No horizontal scroll.
- Draft forms validate on submit and show refusals inline, in the same words as the actions.

## Trust-bar risks

- **Empty states (item 1).** Every active job seeds a subcontract, so no list is empty on seeded data. Harbor shows no entry. A designed empty state still exists for an approved job with no subcontracts: "Add a subcontract to commit sub cost on this job."
- **Nothing announced early (item 1).** No pay application, retention held, or change wording. A grep test over `v5/src/app` and `v5/src/components` fails on "Pay application", "Retention held", or "Sub change order" until 5.2.2 and 5.2.3 change the test.
- **Forecast untouched (item 3).** The M4 and M5 checks on 25-031 pass unchanged. A unit test asserts `itemForecast` and `eacSql` give the same EAC with and without executed subcontracts in 5.2.0.
- **One write path (item 6).** A grep test fails if any file other than `server/subcontracts.ts` and the seed inserts into or updates the four tables.
- **Exact money (item 3).** Amount math is `BigInt` half-up. SQL sums are `bigint`. A must-fail test with a float version of `lineAmountCents` goes red on `lineAmountCents(333, 1_250)`.
- **Page speed (item 1).** Commitment reads are one grouped query. The job page query budget test (`job-page-query-budget.test.ts`) gains at most one query.
- **Reset (item 2).** Reset demo restores all four tables. The reset median stays at or under 1,500 ms.
- **Migration journal.** 5.2.0-a claims **0024**. It merges only after `0021` to `0023` are on `main`, or it renumbers.
