## Decision

Joshua decided on 2026-10-26 (PT):

- A sub can still submit a pay application when its liability insurance certificate has expired. Submission is **not** blocked.
- When a new pay application goes in and the certificate on file has expired, the flow makes the submitter deal with it right then. The first choice is to upload a current certificate in the same flow.
- The reviewer sees the expired or just-renewed state clearly on the pay application.
- Approval is **not** blocked. Do not add an approval hold. That stays true until Joshua says otherwise.

This replaces the intel's recommendation 5 and acceptance check 12, which refused approval after expiry.

In 5.2 the PM enters each pay application from the sub's billing (PRD scope item 6). There is no vendor portal. So the prompt runs for the PM who enters and submits the sub's pay application. If a vendor portal ships later, the same step runs for the sub.

## Why this increment

Paying a sub with no coverage is a real risk for a contractor. A hard stop on submission would hold up billing for a paperwork lapse, and the sub's billing still has to be recorded. Joshua chose a prompt at the moment the pay application goes in, plus a clear state for the reviewer. InEight stores insurance documents with insurer, effective date, and expiration date (C42) and sends expiration notices (C43). Our research did not confirm what InEight does when a payment comes in against an expired certificate (U1). This spec makes no claim either way.

## User stories

1. As the PM, when I submit a pay application for a sub whose liability certificate has expired, the flow stops on an insurance step and asks me to upload the current certificate.
2. As the PM, I upload the renewed certificate in that step, and the pay application submits with "Renewed at submit".
3. As the PM, when the sub has not sent a renewal yet, I can still submit. I must say why, and the pay application records that it went in on an expired certificate.
4. As the PM, when the certificate is current, I see no extra step.
5. As the executive, I open a submitted pay application and see, at the top, whether the certificate was current, renewed at submit, or expired at submit, with carrier, policy number, and dates.
6. As the executive, I can approve a pay application whose certificate was expired at submit. Nothing stops me.
7. As the PM, I see each vendor's certificate history on the subcontract page and open each certificate document.

## Rules

### Certificates

- A certificate belongs to a **vendor**, not to one subcontract. One certificate covers the company on every job. The vendor's subcontracts all read the same certificate.
- Type: `general_liability` only in 5.2.5. Other types are out of scope.
- Fields: carrier, policy number, effective date, expiry date, document link. All required.
- **Certificate on file** = the vendor's `general_liability` certificate with the latest `expires_on`. Ties go to the latest `uploaded_at`.
- **Expired** = `expires_on` is before today in the job's time zone (the same "today" plans use since [#18](https://github.com/jgarrison929/pitbull/pull/18)). A certificate is current through its expiry date.
- **None on file** is treated like expired in the prompt.
- Certificates are append-only. A renewal adds a row. Nothing edits or deletes a certificate in 5.2.5.
- **Upload.** PDF only, checked by the file's first bytes (`%PDF-`), not the name. 5 MB or less. The file is stored in Postgres (`insurance_certificate_files.bytes`). `document_url` points to `/app/insurance/certificates/[id]/file`, which serves the file only to a signed-in user of the same company. No public URL.
- A new certificate is refused when:
  - `expires_on` is before today: "That certificate expired on 2026-11-20. Upload one that is current today."
  - `effective_on` is after today: "That certificate starts on 2026-12-15. Upload the one in force today."
  - `effective_on` is after `expires_on`: "The effective date is after the expiry date."
  - the file is not a PDF or is over 5 MB: "Upload the certificate as a PDF of 5 MB or less."
- Only the PM uploads. The executive and the superintendent read. The foreman sees nothing.

### Submit

- On submit, the server reads the vendor's certificate on file, using the job's today.
- **Current:** submit goes through as in 5.2.2. The pay application records `insurance_at_submit = 'current'`.
- **Expired or none on file:** submit needs an `insurance` answer. Without one, the server returns the step, not a refusal, and writes nothing. The answer is one of:
  - `renew`: a new certificate uploaded in the same flow. The certificate row and the submit commit in one transaction. If the certificate is refused, nothing is written and the step shows the reason. Records `insurance_at_submit = 'renewed'`.
  - `submit_expired`: a reason of 5 to 200 characters. Records `insurance_at_submit = 'expired'` (or `'none'` when nothing was on file) and stores the reason.
- The step is the only way past an expired certificate. There is no setting to skip it and no way to dismiss it without one of the two answers.
- The snapshot is written once at submit: status, certificate id (null for `none`), carrier, policy number, effective and expiry dates, and the reason. It never changes after submit. A later renewal does not rewrite it.
- A rejected pay application that is resubmitted takes a new snapshot at the new submit.
- The same check shows on the draft. When a draft is created or opened for a vendor whose certificate is expired, the draft page shows the insurance line from the review panel with an "Upload current certificate" button. The draft is not blocked.

### Review

- The pay application page shows an **Insurance** panel above the lines, for PM and executive, on every state from `submitted` on.
- Panel text by `insurance_at_submit`:
  - `current`: "Certificate current at submit. General liability, Harborline Casualty, policy GL-55102, effective 2026-04-09, expires 2027-04-09." Neutral tone.
  - `renewed`: "Certificate renewed at submit by Marcus Lee on 2026-12-03. Previous certificate expired on 2026-10-14. New: General liability, …, expires 2027-10-14." Caution tone, with the word "Renewed".
  - `expired`: "Submitted with an expired certificate. General liability, …, expired on 2026-10-14. Reason: Renewal in process per Cascade office." Negative tone, with the word "Expired".
  - `none`: "Submitted with no liability certificate on file. Reason: …" Negative tone, with the words "No certificate".
- Each panel links to the certificate document when there is one.
- When today's certificate on file differs from the snapshot, the panel adds one line: "Today: renewed, expires 2027-10-14" or "Today: expired on 2026-12-01". The snapshot line stays first.
- Color is never the only signal. Every tone carries its word, as 5.0.4-c status badges do.
- The pay application list adds an Insurance column with the same word ("Current", "Renewed", "Expired", "No certificate"). At 320 and 390 px it shows as a badge on the card, not as the main number.
- **Approve is unchanged.** The Approve button is enabled for every `insurance_at_submit` value. The approve event copies the snapshot status into its row so the audit shows what the approver saw.

### Subcontract page

- `/app/jobs/[number]/subcontracts/[sc]` gets an **Insurance** section: the certificate on file with its state word, then older certificates, newest first. Each row: type, carrier, policy number, effective, expiry, uploaded by and when (or "Seeded"), and a link to the document.
- The PM sees "Upload certificate" there too. It uses the same validation as the submit step.
- The superintendent sees the section read-only.

### Typed actions

All writes go through `run()` with `writes: true`. `server/insurance.ts` is the only writer of the two certificate tables.

| Action | Arguments | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `insurance.addCertificate` | `job`, `sc` (to find the vendor), `carrier`, `policyNumber`, `effectiveOn`, `expiresOn`, `file` | pm |
| `insurance.certificates` | `job`, `sc` | pm, executive, superintendent (read) |
| `subPayApp.submit` (from 5.2.2) | adds `insurance`: `{ renew: <addCertificate fields> }` or `{ submitExpired: { reason } }` | pm |

On the assistant rail, "Submit PA-03 on SC-001 for 24-118" with an expired certificate answers: "Cascade Sign & Structure's liability certificate expired on 2026-10-14. Upload a current one on the pay application page, or tell me to submit without one and why." The rail takes the `submitExpired` answer in words. A file upload happens on the screen.

## Data model sketch

Against `v5/src/db/schema.ts` after 5.2.2. Dates are `date` columns, money is unchanged, every row has `company_id`.

```ts
// 5.2.5 (migration 0029). Writer: v5/src/server/insurance.ts
export const insuranceTypeEnum = pgEnum("insurance_type", ["general_liability"]);
export const insuranceAtSubmitEnum = pgEnum("insurance_at_submit", ["current", "renewed", "expired", "none"]);

export const insuranceCertificates = pgTable("insurance_certificates", {
  id: uuid("id").primaryKey(),
  companyId: companyRef(),
  vendorId: uuid("vendor_id").notNull().references(() => vendors.id),
  type: insuranceTypeEnum("type").notNull(),
  carrier: text("carrier").notNull(),
  policyNumber: text("policy_number").notNull(),
  effectiveOn: date("effective_on").notNull(),
  expiresOn: date("expires_on").notNull(),     // check: expires_on >= effective_on
  documentUrl: text("document_url").notNull(), // app route, or a static demo PDF for seed rows
  uploadedBy: uuid("uploaded_by"),             // null for seed rows
  uploadedAt: timestamp("uploaded_at", { withTimezone: true }).notNull().defaultNow(),
});
// index (vendor_id, type, expires_on desc, uploaded_at desc) for "certificate on file"

export const insuranceCertificateFiles = pgTable("insurance_certificate_files", {
  certificateId: uuid("certificate_id").primaryKey().references(() => insuranceCertificates.id, { onDelete: "cascade" }),
  companyId: companyRef(),
  contentType: text("content_type").notNull(), // always application/pdf in 5.2.5
  sizeBytes: integer("size_bytes").notNull(),  // check: 1 to 5,242,880
  bytes: bytea("bytes").notNull(),
});

// sub_pay_apps gains the snapshot, written once at submit (writer stays server/sub-pay-apps.ts):
//   insurance_at_submit        insurance_at_submit, null until submitted
//   insurance_certificate_id   uuid, nullable, references insurance_certificates
//   insurance_carrier, insurance_policy_number, insurance_effective_on, insurance_expires_on
//   insurance_reason           text, required when insurance_at_submit in ('expired', 'none')
// check: insurance_at_submit is not null when status <> 'draft'
// sub_pay_app_events gains insurance_at_submit (copied on submit and approve rows)
```

The snapshot copies the certificate fields rather than only pointing at the row. The review panel reads one row, and the audit does not depend on joins.

### CI only

1. `isExpired(expiresOn, todayInJobZone)` is false on the expiry date and true the day after. A must-fail version that compares UTC timestamps goes red on a fixture where the job's today is one day behind UTC.
2. `certificateOnFile` picks the latest `expires_on`, then the latest `uploaded_at`, on a fixture with three rows.
3. The submit path with an expired certificate and no `insurance` answer writes no row in any table. With `renew` and a refused file, it writes no row.
4. Approve succeeds for each `insurance_at_submit` value. A must-fail version of approve that refuses on `expired` goes red.
5. A grep test fails if any file other than `server/insurance.ts` and the seed inserts into `insurance_certificates` or `insurance_certificate_files`, or if any file other than `server/sub-pay-apps.ts` writes the `insurance_*` columns on `sub_pay_apps`.
6. The upload check accepts a fixture starting with `%PDF-` and refuses a PNG with a `.pdf` name.
7. The `0029` migration test asserts both enums and the `insurance_at_submit` not-null check on non-draft pay applications.

## Out of scope

- Any block on approval or on submission for an expired certificate. That needs a new decision from Joshua.
- Certificate types other than general liability: auto, umbrella, workers' comp, and additional-insured endorsements.
- Coverage limits, deductibles, and checking limits against a subcontract requirement.
- Checking a certificate with the carrier or a broker. Pitbull records what was uploaded.
- Expiration notices, reminder emails, and a dashboard of certificates about to expire.
- A vendor portal where the sub uploads its own pay application or certificate.
- Surety bonds, lien waivers (U4), licenses, tax forms, and other supporting documents. Document control is 5.3.
- Editing or deleting a certificate. File types other than PDF. Files over 5 MB.
- A per-subcontract certificate requirement or override.

## Trust-bar risks

- **Approval stays open (item 6).** The decision is a prompt, not a hold. CI check 4 and acceptance check 3 fail if approval ever refuses on insurance. No label says "hold" or "blocked".
- **Honest words (item 1).** Labels say "Certificate on file", "Current", "Renewed", "Expired", "No certificate". No screen says the sub "is insured" or "is compliant". Pitbull only knows what was uploaded.
- **Dates in the job's zone (item 3).** Expiry compares dates in the job's time zone, like plan "today" since #18. CI check 1 guards the off-by-one.
- **Empty states (item 1).** Every seeded vendor with an executed subcontract has a certificate, so the Insurance section is never empty on seeded data. A designed empty state still exists: "No liability certificate on file for Valley Deck Finishers. Upload one."
- **Uploaded files (item 2).** Files are scoped to the sandbox company, served only through the app route, and deleted on Reset demo and sandbox expiry. No seeded file rows, so the reset median stays at or under 1,500 ms.
- **Snapshot is final (item 6).** The submit snapshot never changes. Today's state shows on its own line, so the reviewer sees both.
- **Nothing announced early (item 4).** No notice, reminder, or "coming soon" text for the out-of-scope items.
- **Page speed (item 1).** The panel reads the snapshot on the pay application row. The certificate on file is one indexed query per page. Pages stay under 1.5 s on staging.
- **Migration journal.** 5.2.5-a claims **0029**. It merges after `0028` if 5.2.4 has one, or renumbers.
