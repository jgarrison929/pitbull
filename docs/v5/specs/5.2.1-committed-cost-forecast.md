## Why this increment

A commitment that does not move the forecast is a list, not project control. InEight's Committed Cost method sets forecast final cost to total committed cost and forecast remaining cost to open committed cost (M3). Once a sub scope is bought out, the commitment is a better estimate of final cost than the budget. This is the number a PM shows after "what did we buy that scope for?"

## User stories

1. As the PM, I switch Cedar's 05-120 to Committed Cost, and its EAC becomes the executed commitment, with the formula shown.
2. As the PM, I see uncommitted Current Budget next to EAC, never inside it.
3. As the PM, I cannot pick Committed Cost on a cost item with no executed subcontract.
4. As the executive, I approve an owner change order on a committed item. Current Budget moves, EAC does not, and the job page says the change is not yet covered by a commitment.
5. As the executive, I see the job and portfolio EAC move by exactly the per-item change.

## Rules

- `forecast_method` gains `committed_cost`, labelled **Committed Cost**.
- **EAC** for a `committed_cost` item = `locked_actual_cents` + open commitment. **ETC** = open commitment. In 5.2.1 there are no pay applications, so open commitment = commitment. When the item has no other cost, EAC = executed commitment (M3).
- `itemForecast` gains a `commitmentCents` input and `eacSql` gains a branch that joins the same grouped commitment. They agree to the cent on every fixture.
- **Derivation string**, exact: `EAC $2,243,000.00 = AC $0.00 + open commitment $2,243,000.00 (Committed Cost)`.
- **Uncommitted Current Budget** = Current Budget − commitment. It shows beside EAC on the cost item page and in the job cost table's detail drawer. It never enters EAC or ETC (recommendation 3, I1).
- **Who can switch.** The PM only, as today. Switching to `committed_cost` is refused when the item has no executed subcontract line: "04-210 has no executed subcontract. Committed Cost needs one." Summary codes stay refused as today.
- Switching back to Current Budget or Average Performance is allowed at any time. Each switch writes the existing audit entry, naming the old and new method.
- **Owner change orders.** On approval, `change_order_events.eac_delta_cents` is computed from the method-aware forecast before and after. For a `committed_cost` item, a CB change gives an EAC delta of $0.00. Current Budget and Average Performance items behave as on `main`.
- **Not covered by a commitment.** The job page lists each approved owner change order line on a `committed_cost` item that was approved after that item's latest subcontract execution: CO number, cost item, and amount. It also shows that item's uncommitted Current Budget. In 5.2.1 nothing clears the entry except a new executed subcontract line on that item that brings uncommitted CB to $0.00 or less. 5.2.3 adds coverage by sub change orders (recommendation 6).
- **Executing on a committed item.** Executing another subcontract with lines on a `committed_cost` item raises its EAC by those lines' amount in the same transaction. The `execute` event records the EAC delta.
- Seed: every item keeps its 5.0 method. No seeded item uses `committed_cost`.
