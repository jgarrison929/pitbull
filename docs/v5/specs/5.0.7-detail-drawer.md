# 5.0.7: detail drawer

Status: build-ready spec, written 2026-10-27 (PT) against `main` at `05a1b710` and `rc/5.0.4` at `c042a959` (Next 16.4.2, React 19.3.1, `radix-ui` 1.8). Source: Pitbull UX, `03-spec-detail-drawer.md` (audit items REPORT Top 10 #4 and flow F2). Target release **2026-11-09**. See the [roadmap](../../roadmap.md).

Trust bar item 5 names the detail drawer as the last part of the page anatomy. Today there is none. Every drill-down is a full page, and the user loses the job page and its scroll position.

UI only. No backend change, no data model change, no EV math change, and no migration. The one new route is a read-only page, `/app/jobs/[number]/change-orders/[co]`, so a change order has a canonical URL.

## User stories

1. As the PM on a job, I open a cost code, read its forecast and entries, step to the next code, and close it. The job page and its table position are still there.
2. As the PM, I approve CO-001 from its drawer and see the budget move without leaving the job.
3. As the superintendent on a phone, I open a plan line full screen, remove a line with a confirm step, and go back to the job.
4. As anyone with a link, I paste a cost item, CO, or plan URL into a new tab and get the full page.

## Rules

### One component, three kinds

`DetailDrawer` in `v5/src/components/ui/detail-drawer.tsx` shows three kinds of detail from the job page:

| Kind | Opens from | Canonical URL | Content source today |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cost item | `cost-code-link-{code}` in `CostTable` | `/app/jobs/{number}/cost-items/{code}` | `cost-items/[code]/page.tsx` |
| Change order | The CO card heading link in `ChangeOrdersSection` | `/app/jobs/{number}/change-orders/{co}` | `change-orders.tsx`. The page is new |
| Plan line | A line in `PlanCard`, or the plan title link | `/app/jobs/{number}/plans/{id}#line-{lineId}` | `plans/[id]/page.tsx` |

### Routing

- The job route gets a `@drawer` parallel slot with `(.)cost-items/[code]`, `(.)change-orders/[co]`, and `(.)plans/[id]` intercepts.
- A soft navigation from the job page (a `next/link` click) renders the drawer over the job. The address bar shows the canonical URL.
- A hard load, a new tab, or a refresh of the canonical URL renders the full page, with its back link (`cost-item-back`, `plan-detail-back`, `co-detail-back`).
- No query parameters. `#line-{lineId}` on a plan URL selects and outlines one line.
- The 5.0.4-a anchors (`#plan-{id}`, `#co-CO-001`) on the job URL keep working and do not open the drawer. Moving next-step links to drawer URLs is a later change.
- Drawer links use `next/link` with `prefetch={false}`. `page.prefetch.test.ts` keeps the server from loading every cost item on view; 24-077 alone renders 40+ code links.
- Unknown code or CO on a known job: HTTP 404 on a hard load, and the not-found state in the drawer.

### Shape

| Width | Shape | Size | Scrim | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1440 | Right panel over `main` and the assistant rail | 560 px wide, full height | `bg-chrome/40` | 160 ms slide from the right, none under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` |
| 834 | Right panel | `min(560px, 100vw − 48px)` | Same | Same |
| 640 and below | Full-screen sheet | 100vw × 100dvh | none | 160 ms slide up, none under reduced motion |

Top to bottom: a 56 px sticky header (Close, title `h2`, and at 640 and up Previous and Next, each 44×44); a one-sentence summary used as `aria-describedby`; up to 4 `MetricCard`s in a 2 × 2 grid (1 column at 320); `h3` sections; a 64 px sticky footer with the role's one primary action, only when the role has one; and **Open full page** (`detail-drawer-full-page`), at least 44 px tall.

Exact cents are allowed on drawer cards, because each card is at least 248 px wide at 560. Every figure must still pass "no clip".

Per kind:

- **Cost item.** Cards: EAC with `forecastDerivation` as its secondary, Current budget, EV, AC with "Through {periodLabel} lock" or "{amount} pending lock". Sections: "Forecast" (`cost-item-forecast`, the PM's `ForecastMethodControl`, read-only text for other roles), "How it earns" (`earn-derivation`, moved here from the cost row), "Quantities", "Cost entries" (`cost-item-entries-cards`, split by `cost-entries-since-lock-heading` and `cost-entries-through-lock-heading`, total in `cost-item-totals`).
- **Change order.** Cards: total, Current Budget change, EAC change (from `co-preview`), status as a word. Sections: "Lines" (`co-lines-stack` below 640, `co-lines-table` at 640 and up when it fits), "Reason", "History". Footer by state and role: **Price** (draft), **Approve** (priced), with **Reject** and **Void** as secondary buttons whose reason fields (`co-reject-reason`, `co-void-reason`) validate first.
- **Plan line.** The drawer shows the whole plan and scrolls to `#line-{lineId}`, which gets a 2 px `--ring` outline. Cards: planned MH, reported MH, earned MH, gain or loss in MH. Sections: "Lines" (`plan-detail-line` cards), "History" (`plan-history`, `plan-history-event`). Footer: **Submit plan**, **Report day's work**, or **Approve report**, by role and state.

### The change order page

`/app/jobs/[number]/change-orders/[co]/page.tsx` is new and read-only for every role. It reads the same CO data the job page loader returns and adds no query shape.

- Title "{co} · {CO title}", for example "CO-001 · Owner-added retaining wall, north approach". Purpose line: "One change order: its lines, what it does to the budget, and its history." (72 characters, within the 5.0.4-b limit.)
- It shows the same cards and sections as the CO drawer, with no footer action. A role that can act on the CO gets a link to `/app/jobs/{number}#co-{co}` ("Act on {co} on the job page"). Every role gets `co-detail-back` to the job. So the page is never a dead end.
- **Foreman.** CO cards on the job page are not linked for the foreman. On a direct load the foreman gets HTTP 200 with the CO number, title, status word, and reason, and no money: no total, no Current Budget change, no EAC change, and no line amounts. That keeps the 5.0.4-b foreman rule (no dollar delta anywhere).
- **Superintendent.** Read only, as on the job page. No `-cpi` or `-spi` element, per the 5.0.4-b superintendent rule.

### Crawl set

The CO page joins the Verify crawl, so it is never an off-crawl page under the 5.0.4-g rule (5.0.4-g check 1 allows only paths in `navCrawlPages`, `drillItemList`, and `/login`).

- `navCrawlPages` in `.cursor/skills/verify-v5/crawl.mjs` adds `/app/jobs/{job}/change-orders/{co}` for every change order in the seed, whatever its state. After reset that is 6 pages: CO-001 on 25-031 (priced), CO-001 and CO-002 on 24-077, and CO-001, CO-002 (rejected), and CO-003 on 24-118. The list comes from the seed, not from this spec.
- The crawl loads each one for all 4 roles, at every crawl width, in light and dark, with the same per-page checks as every other crawled page.
- The cost-item and plan URLs are already crawled (`drillItemList` and the plan-page loads), so no change there.
- A hard load is what the crawl tests. The drawer is tested by checks D1 to D29.

### Roles

The drawer's URL is the cost-item, CO, or plan URL, so the 5.0.4-b rules for those pages apply inside the drawer too.

| Kind | Executive | PM | Superintendent | Foreman |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cost item | Read. No forecast control | Forecast control (`costItem.setForecastMethod`). PM percent and milestones | Read figures with no CPI or SPI value. PM percent and milestones as `actions.ts` allows | Quantities and cost entries. No CPI, no $ delta, no forecast section |
| Change order | Price, Approve, Reject, Void | Same | Read | Not opened from the job page. Direct load: the money-free page above |
| Plan line | Read | Read | Edit, Remove (ConfirmDialog), Submit, Approve, Return | Edit reported values, Report day's work, Submit plan |

The UX source let the superintendent read the cost-item drawer "with CPI". That breaks 5.0.4-b check 3, which bans CPI and SPI values on cost-item pages for the superintendent. The 5.0.4 rule wins: the superintendent's cost-item drawer and summary carry no CPI.

### Keyboard and screen reader

| Event | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Open | The trigger is a real `<a href>` to the canonical URL. Enter opens it. Ctrl or Cmd click and middle click open the full page in a new tab. A pointer click anywhere on a cost row runs the same link. Keyboard users tab to the code link only |
| Focus on open | Focus moves to the title `h2` (`tabIndex={-1}`), not to the first input. Radix `onOpenAutoFocus` is overridden for this |
| While open | `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, built on Radix `Dialog`. Focus stays inside. Content behind is `inert`. Tab order: Close, Previous, Next, content, footer action, Open full page |
| Esc | Closes the drawer. If a `ConfirmDialog` is open inside it, Esc closes only the ConfirmDialog, and focus returns to its trigger inside the drawer. A second Esc closes the drawer |
| Scrim click | Closes the drawer, unless a field has unsaved text. Then `ConfirmDialog` opens: title "Discard the reason you typed for {co}?", body "The text is not saved anywhere.", verb **Discard**, destructive |
| Close | `router.back()` after a soft navigation, so the job URL and scroll position come back |
| Focus on close | Returns to the link that opened it. The cost table is virtualized, so it scrolls that row into view first. If the row is filtered out, focus goes to `cost-tree-filter` |
| Previous and Next | Move to the neighboring leaf in the current filtered and expanded order with `router.replace`, so Back still closes the drawer. Names include the target: "Next cost code: 07-410". At the ends they are `aria-disabled="true"`, still focusable, with the reason in `title` |
| Action result | A `role="status"` region announces the outcome, for example "CO-001 approved. Current Budget increased by $60,000.00." The figures refresh in place and the drawer stays open |
| Headings | `h2` for the title, `h3` for sections. No skipped levels |
| Zoom | At 400% zoom (320 CSS px), the full-screen sheet layout. Nothing scrolls sideways |

### ConfirmDialog inside the drawer

Every irreversible action in the drawer runs through `ConfirmForm`, which opens `ConfirmDialog` with copy from `lib/confirm-copy.ts`. The drawer adds no confirm of its own.

- Covered: CO Approve, Reject, and Void; plan line Remove; daily report Approve and Return.
- Stacking: ConfirmDialog's overlay and content are `z-50`. The drawer uses `z-40`. Both portal to `body`.
- Reasons (reject, void, return) validate on the drawer form before the dialog opens.
- Lock Period and Approve estimate stay in the job page header ([5.0.6](5.0.6-job-page-anatomy.md), 5.0.4-h).

## States

| State | Trigger | What shows |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | Click, until the RSC payload arrives | Panel and header within 100 ms. The title uses the row's code and description, which the client has. Cards and sections show skeletons at final size |
| Loaded | Payload arrives | Content as above |
| Empty list | No cost entries, no CO lines, or no plan history | The `list-empty.tsx` helper for that list, for example "No cost posted on 07-300" |
| Draft estimate | `estimateStatus === "draft"` (26-010) | Cards show the dash glyph with "After estimate approval". No `earn-derivation` or `forecast-derivation`. The forecast control is disabled with "Forecast method opens after the estimate is approved." No "$0.00" and no "EAC $0 = $0 + ($0 − $0)" |
| Locked period | `periodLabel` set | AC secondary "Through {periodLabel} lock". Entries split into "Since the last lock" and "Through {periodLabel}". Figures match the job KPI strip to the cent |
| Action pending | A form posts | The button shows its `pendingLabel` and is disabled |
| Action refused | `approveChangeOrderCopy` returns a `refusal` | The ConfirmDialog body shows the refusal and has no verb button |
| Not found | Code or CO no longer exists | Title "{code} is not on {job number}". Body "It may have been removed by an estimate import." Close |
| Error | Payload fails | Title keeps the code. Body "{code} did not load. Your data is unchanged." **Try again** (44 px) and **Open full page** |
| Offline (foreman report) | No network on a phone | The field intel check 10 offline queue applies unchanged. The drawer adds no storage |

## Out of scope

- A pinned, non-modal drawer on very wide screens.
- Drawers outside the job page (portfolio rows, the changes log).
- Editing estimate lines in the drawer.
- Moving the 5.0.4-a next-step links to drawer URLs.
- The draft treatment on the full cost-item page (UX fix 6). D21 covers the drawer only.

## Trust-bar risks

- **Page speed (item 1).** The drawer loads one item's payload on click, not on view (`prefetch={false}`). The job page must stay under 1.5 s with the `@drawer` slot added, and each canonical URL under 1.5 s on a hard load (D5, C1). Land after #70 so the drawer's loads are measured on the faster pool.
- **Numbers to the cent (item 3).** Drawer figures come from the same loaders as the full pages. D11 and D20 compare them to the cent.
- **Role rules.** The drawer URL is a cost-item, CO, or plan URL, so the 5.0.4-b role rules apply inside it. D22, D29, C4, and C5 guard them.
- **Crawl.** A new route outside the crawl would fail 5.0.4-g check 1 the first time any panel links to it. C1 puts it in the crawl first.
- **Baselines.** The job page and CO page get light and dark baselines in `v5/tests/e2e/visual.spec.ts` in the same PR.
