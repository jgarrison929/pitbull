# 5.0.6: job page anatomy

Status: build-ready spec, written 2026-10-27 (PT) against `main` at `05a1b710` and `rc/5.0.4` at `c042a959`. Source: Pitbull UX, `02-spec-job-page-anatomy.md` (audit item REPORT Top 10 #2, flow F1). Target release **2026-11-04**. See the [roadmap](../../roadmap.md).

This spec changes the order, grouping, and copy of `/app/jobs/[number]`. It changes no backend, no data model, and no EV math. Every figure named here is a field `timedLoadJobPage` already returns.

## Why this increment

Trust bar item 5 asks for one page anatomy: header, KPIs, primary table, detail. The job page today runs in this order:

1. `PageHeader` (name, number, owner, PM, finish, Approve estimate or Lock Period, status pill)
2. The `estimate-status` sentence
3. `ImportEstimateForm` (draft only, PM)
4. `JobNewPlanSlot`, then every open `PlanCard` with its forms
5. `plan-history`
6. `KpiStrip`: up to 8 tiles, which wrap 4 + 3 at 1440
7. The `estimate-categories` line, or "Lump budget …"
8. "Field measurement methods" (`EvMethodControls`)
9. `CostTable` with a 4-line meta paragraph
10. `ChangeOrdersSection`
11. "Changes on this job"

In the Pitbull UX audit (staging 5.0.3, PM on 24-077 at 1440), the KPIs start about 770 px down and the first cost row about 1,500 px down. At 390 the page is 5,414 px tall. The PM's question, "is this job on budget, and which cost codes drive it?", is answered two screens below the field forms.

## User stories

1. As the PM at 1440, I see the job's KPIs and the first cost codes without scrolling.
2. As the executive, I see the job's status and its five KPIs in the first screen.
3. As the superintendent or the foreman on a phone, my plan card is still the first thing under the header, so the 5.0.4 journeys stay at 0 px scroll.
4. As any role, the header names the one thing I should do next on this job.

## Rules

### Order

The order is the same for every role and width. Content in each slot differs by role (see [Roles](#roles)).

| # | Slot | Contents | testid |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Header | `h1` job name. Meta line: number · owner · PM · finish. Status. At most one primary action. One "Next for you" line when the role has an item on this job | `page-header`, `job-status`, `job-primary-action`, `job-next-for-you` |
| 2 | KPI strip | 5 `MetricCard`s (3 for the foreman), then a "More figures" `<details>` | `job-kpis`, `job-more-figures` |
| 3 | Primary table | `CostTable`, titled "Cost codes" | `cost-table` |
| 4 | Work panel | The role's work: daily plans, change orders, field measurement, changes on this job, in role order | `job-work` |

Exception, from 5.0.4: below 1024 px, the superintendent's and the foreman's open plan card sits between the header and the KPI strip. That keeps the 5.0.4 journeys at 0 px scroll on a phone. At 1024 and up, the header action and the `#plan-<id>` anchor do the same work.

### Header

- **Status.** Approved job: `StatusPill` with the word and the dollar variance ("At risk −$255K" on 24-077). Draft job: `draft-label` "Draft estimate". Status is always a word plus color.
- **Primary action.** At most one filled `--brand` button per role and job state (see [Roles](#roles)). Other actions sit in the work panel.
- **Next for you.** The first `getNextSteps` item for this job, with the same label text as on `/app` (5.0.4-a), prefixed "Next for you: ". Hidden when the role has no item on this job. No "Nothing to do" text.
- The `estimate-status` sentence moves into the status pill's `title`. The element and its testid stay in the DOM so the existing checks still read it.

### KPI strip

Exactly 5 cards for the PM, the executive, and the superintendent. Each card has 1 primary figure and at most 2 secondary figures (the 5.0.4-c `MetricCard` rule).

| Card | testid | Primary | Secondary | Fields |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cost performance (CPI) | `job-cpi` | index, 2 decimals, with word | Earned, Actual (`usdShort`) | `p.cpi`, `p.earnedCents`, `p.actualCents` |
| Schedule performance (SPI) | `job-spi` | index, 2 decimals, with word | Planned value (PV) (`usdShort`), "as of {lock date}" | `p.spi`, `p.plannedCents`, `p.lockAt` |
| Current budget | `job-budget` | `usdShort` | Original budget, % earned | `p.budgetCents`, `jobMoney.obCents`, `p.percent` |
| Estimate at completion (EAC) | `job-eac` | `usdShort` | "Sum of cost-code forecasts" | `p.eacCents` |
| Cost variance (CV) | `job-cv` | signed `usdShort`, U+2212 for negative | Schedule variance (SV), signed | `p.costVarianceCents`, `p.scheduleVarianceCents` |

- Every short figure carries `title` and `data-cents` with the exact value.
- At 1440: one row of 5 equal columns, 16 px gap. No wrap at 1280 and up. At 834: 3 columns then 2. At 390: EAC and CPI first (5.0.4-e), then SPI and Current budget, then Cost variance.
- **More figures** (`job-more-figures`) is a native `<details>` under the strip, with a `summary` at least 44 px tall. It holds Current estimate (`jobMoney.ceCents`), MH budgeted (`jobMoney.mhMilli`), the estimate categories, and the since-lock figures. These testids stay on the same values: `job-estimate`, `estimate-labor`, `estimate-material`, `estimate-equipment`, `estimate-sub`, `estimate-mh`, `job-actual-since-lock`, `job-original-budget`, `job-earned`, `job-actual`. `numbers.mjs` reads `data-cents` with `getAttribute`, which works on a closed `<details>`. Any Verify step that needs visible text opens the `<details>` first.

### Cost table

- Section title "Cost codes". The section meta is one sentence (see [Copy](#copy)). The 4-line meta paragraph with its color legend goes. Color meaning comes from the words next to each status value (5.0.4-c check 6).
- The filter, the value-set switch (`value-set-ob`, `value-set-cb`, `value-set-ce`), and the virtualized scroll stay.
- Roll-up rows show `usdShort` with `title` and `data-cents`. Leaf rows keep exact cents at 1024 and up only if every column passes "no clip". Otherwise they use `usdShort` with `title` and `data-cents`.
- `earn-derivation` and `forecast-derivation` stay in the row in 5.0.6. They move into the detail drawer in [5.0.7](5.0.7-detail-drawer.md), which needs the drawer to exist first.
- A row tap opens the full cost-item page, as today. 5.0.7 turns it into the drawer.

### Work panel

- Sections in role order (see [Roles](#roles)). Each keeps its anchor id so the 5.0.4-a links still land: `id="plan-<id>"` on each `PlanCard`, `id="co-<number>"` on each CO card, and `id="estimate"` on the header block that holds **Approve estimate**.
- Removed from the screen: "Lump budget — each cost item is one seeded total, with no labor, material, equipment, or subcontract split." The `estimate-status` sentence moves to the pill `title`.

### Confirm steps

Lock Period and Approve estimate keep the ConfirmDialog from 5.0.4-h ([#71](https://github.com/jgarrison929/pitbull/pull/71)). This spec does not change their copy. If #71's copy differs from the examples in check J14 or J15, #71 wins and the checks follow it.

## Roles

Capabilities come from `caps` in `server/job-page.ts` and the role lists in `server/assistant/actions.ts`. Role-fit rules come from `lib/role-fit.ts` (`showsJobPageCostIndexes`, `showsStatusRiskBadge`) and the [5.0.4-b rules](5.0.4-clear-next-steps-and-mobile-polish.md#504-b-page-purpose-lines-and-role-fit-metrics):

- The superintendent sees CPI and SPI values (`[data-testid$="-cpi"]`, `[data-testid$="-spi"]`) on `/app/jobs/[number]` only.
- The foreman sees no CPI or SPI value, no dollar risk badge, and no dollar delta anywhere.

| | Executive (Elena Brooks) | PM (Marcus Lee) | Superintendent (Ray Dominguez) | Foreman (Luis Ortega) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Header status | Pill with word and $ variance | Same | Same | Today's plan state from `todayPlanLabel`: "Report due", "Reported", or "No plan today". No $ and no "At risk" |
| Primary action | Lock {period} (approved), Approve estimate (draft) | Same, plus Import estimate on a draft | Submit plan on a draft plan, Approve report on a reported plan, else New plan | Report day's work on an issued plan, Submit plan on a draft plan |
| KPI strip | 5 cards | 5 cards | 5 cards, including `job-cpi` and `job-spi` (job page only) | 3 cards, below |
| Cost table at ≥ 1024 | Full | Full | Full, with CPI | No CPI column, no variance column, no signed $ figure |
| Cost table at < 1024 | Full | Full | Plan card moves above the KPI strip | Plan card with the report form moves above the KPI strip. Rows show no CPI and no signed $ figure |
| Work panel order | Change orders, Daily plans, Field measurement, Changes | Change orders, Field measurement, Daily plans, Changes | Daily plans, Change orders (read), Changes | Daily plans, Changes |

**Foreman KPI strip.** 3 cards, with no `-cpi` or `-spi` testid, no status tone, and no signed figure:

| Card | testid | Primary | Secondary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Today's plan | `job-today-plan` | Planned MH from the open plan's lines | Crew, `todayPlanLabel` |
| Job % complete | `job-percent` | `p.percent`, whole number | none |
| Current budget | `job-budget` | `usdShort` of `p.budgetCents`, with `title` and `data-cents` | none |

The UX source gives the foreman a Current budget card. It stays. The 5.0.4 foreman rule bans CPI and SPI values, the dollar risk badge, and the dollar delta (5.0.4-b check 2: `/At risk/` and `/[−-]\$\d/`). A budget total is none of those. In this spec the card carries no variance, no Original budget secondary, and no status tone, so it shows no delta in figures or in color. Check J25 guards that.

## Trust-bar risks

- **Page speed (item 1).** The page renders the same data in a new order. `<details>` content still renders on the server, so payload size does not drop. Keep every job page under 1.5 s (J18). Stay out of the loader's query shape so #70's pool and cold-path work is not undone.
- **Numbers to the cent (item 3).** The KPI tiles move and some go into More figures. J20 keeps M2, M4, and M5 on the same testids and `data-cents`.
- **Journeys (item 5).** The 5.0.4 journeys must stay at their tap limits with 0 px scroll. J6, J7, J8, and J23 guard that.
- **Baselines.** The job page baselines in `v5/tests/e2e/visual.spec.ts` change in both themes in the same PR.
